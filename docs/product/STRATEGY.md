# Product strategy

_Draft written by Claude from README.md, DESIGN.md and the build so far. **The owner edits this; the
`/product` skill only proposes changes** (as a 🙋 decision in the roadmap). Everything below is a
hypothesis until a conversation with the owner confirms it._

## Who it's for

People with many interests who don't want goal-setting apps. They dabble, drift and return. They
want to see where their energy actually goes and to pick an interest back up without guilt.

## What it should feel like (north star)

1. **Logging is effortless.** One line, any time, from anywhere.
2. **Returning is warm.** Whatever you drifted from greets you with "you left off here" and a small
   next step, never with "you broke your streak".
3. **Know the why; leave the how open.** What you're moving toward — intents, values — can be named
   up front or surface from what you do; the app keeps it in view either way. How you get there
   stays open: impulses, inclinations and detours are how paths are found, not failures to follow a
   plan. _(Owner decision D-4, 2026-11-21.)_
4. **It's yours.** Data stays on your device, survives anything, and leaves with you whenever you
   want.

## Refusals

DESIGN.md's *never* list is the product's identity, not a constraint to work around: no goals,
streaks, % complete, timers, notifications, badge counts, and nothing created or attached without a
tap. An idea borrowed from another app is changed to fit these, or it isn't used.

No schedules, deadlines or progress toward a goal _(reworded by the owner, D-13, 2026-11-23: was
"no steps-to-goal, milestones or schedules"; a landmark is a wander's name, not a target)_ — a next step is one optional crumb,
never a plan. _(Owner decision D-4, 2026-11-21.)_ A step can hold a smaller step toward it, one at a
time: nothing is planned ahead, nothing is counted, only the top is ever staged. _(D-5.)_

## Themes (current bets)

Each theme is a direction for discovery and roadmap items, ordered by the draft's guess at value.
Re-ordering them is a product decision.

| Theme | The bet | Examples already on the roadmap or Later |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | People only log their life where it's safe | P-01 persistence, P-04 import, P-05 Safari/Firefox, P-11 native crash safety |
| **Capture anywhere** | The fewer steps to log, the truer the picture | P-03 `@` at the cursor, share-in from other apps, a home-screen widget |
| **Warm return** | Coming back should feel like being welcomed | crumbs, Earlier, opt-in "On this day" |
| **Seeing flow** | Insight comes from what flows into what | value headers, graph tuning with real data, search |
| **Evidence** | Some things are better remembered with a picture | photo evidence and a per-thread gallery |

## How we know it's working

There's no analytics, by design. The signal is **the owner's own use**, as they describe it in
conversations (logged in [`owner-notes.md`](owner-notes.md)), plus the quality bar: no data loss,
the whole definition of done passing, and accessibility checks staying green.
