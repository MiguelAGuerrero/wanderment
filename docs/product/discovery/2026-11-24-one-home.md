# One home: the sky is the home, a session is a look up (2026-11-24)

Owner: "how these two views can be unified by looking how adjacent apps would do something similar …
one home view with a log button, and then the home view just launches you into the session … like a
video game home screen." Three product scouts (reports, local: `.polish/product/discovery-home-
{games,calm-apps,sessions}.md`). **Source caveat:** the session's network blocked many primary sites
(forestapp.cc, help.headspace.com, apps.apple.com, Wikipedia, Fandom, docs.expo.dev, docs.swmansion.com
and others); most claims rest on search summaries, labelled in the reports. Apple's HIG Modality page
and the installed `node_modules` were read directly.

## What the three territories agree on
1. **The home is a place, not a menu, and the result of a session lands in it.** Journey's title
   sits over the desert you stand in; Sky: Children of Light's home is a place; Forest adds the tree
   to your forest; Oak's tree sprouts; Duolingo's home is the path; How We Feel animates a finished
   check-in back to its card. For Wanderment: **the sky is the home**; after Return, Done it, ↳ First
   step, Toward… — or just leaving — the view eases back down to the lantern with the new star in its
   fixed place overhead.
2. **One way in.** Daylio, Day One, Apple Journal: one ＋. Journey: the world is the title screen.
   For Wanderment the "log button" is **the field itself** (already live; typing anywhere on web
   starts it) — a separate ＋ would add a tap to every log.
3. **A session is one focused layer over the home, with one way out** (Apple HIG Modality: a
   hierarchy inside a modal makes people "forget how to retrace their steps"; iA Writer's focus
   mode). Your star's words as the heading; Done it, ↳ First step, the current step with Did it,
   Toward… — one path, everything else dimmed; Esc/Back/swipe down returns, focus back to the star.
   This merges "under a star" and "on the trail" for a single pursuit.
4. **Coming back shows the world as it is — never absence.** Finch (no visual record of time away),
   Kinder World (plants never die), Outer Wilds (every loop starts at the same campfire, no time
   left shown). Counter-examples refused: Forest's withered trees, Animal Crossing's weeds and
   "your island misses you", idle games' "Welcome back" tallies.
5. **Resume opens exactly where you left off, no question first** (Kindle, Spotify). Tapping a lit
   star opens its session at the current step with your note.

## Doesn't fit (don't propose again)
Forest's withering, coins and group sessions; any session timer (Forest, Session, Sunsama,
Structured, Flow, Opal); Live Activities / persistent session notifications; Headspace's daily
checklist; post-session rating or reflection pop-ups (Calm); distraction blocking; continue shelves
with progress bars; Duolingo's streaks/XP/hearts; Finch's energy bar, daily goal and timed adventure;
Apple Journal streak insights; "constellations revealed as you stay consistent"; How We Feel's weekly
review threshold and Day One's On This Day surfacing by itself; mood-axis check-ins (owner: words
only); Stoic's morning/evening entry points; Year in Pixels (a third view); Pause Mode; a "Continue"
button on the home; end-of-session summaries; a save gesture.

## Tooling (Expo SDK 58, RN 0.88.1, expo-router 58.0.32, Reanimated 4.7.1)
- **Recommended:** keep one home route and drive a single camera value (0 ground → 1 sky) with the
  `Animated` + react-native-svg already used by P-72's `passage.tsx`; draw the sky once (root layout or
  the home) so it never unmounts. No new dependency; same on web, iOS, Android.
- expo-router modal/formSheet render as plain stack pages on web (use `transparentModal` if needed).
- Reanimated 4.7.1 is installed but unused in `src/`; shared-element transitions default-on only from
  4.8 and web support unconfirmed — not needed for one route.
- Skia: native dependency, ~2.9 MB gzipped WASM on web — not now.
- The View Transitions API is web-only; not the main mechanism.
